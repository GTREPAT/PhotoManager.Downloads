WHAT'S NEW · 1.0.0.24

PhotoManager 1.0.0.24

Version 1.0.0.24 is now available. Released on October 11, 2026.

This update expands photo editing and makes large collections easier to manage. These notes describe the final features, not intermediate development stages.

RAW processing and image quality
  • High-precision decoding, floating-point adjustments and intermediate caches, linear-light white balance and use of partially clipped highlight data where the RAW file retains information.
  • Luminance and color noise reduction, sharpening with noise protection and draggable 100% inspection. More consistent color between editor and thumbnails, plus compatibility fixes for Canon EOS R1 and Nikon Z9 previews.
LUTs and presets
  • LUT library with previews, categories and favorites; multiple .cube import and management in Configuración. Adjustable strength and global, masked or batch application while preserving other adjustments.
  • Block-based presets with sample thumbnails, comparison before applying and single-step undo. Rename, duplicate, delete and import/export with their LUTs. Deleting a preset does not remove edits already applied.
Light, color and curves
  • A single Auto proposes light, contrast and vibrance adjustments based on the current edit. Review values and strength, cancel or apply with single-step undo, without changing white balance. Temporary block comparison and clipping warnings linked to the adjustment.
  • Adjust HSL by clicking and dragging on the photo, with range width, softness and an indicative highlight overlay. Curves can locate the tone under the pointer and add or select points from the image.
Crop, perspective and export
  • Straighten using a horizon line and correct perspective with four adjustable guide points. Preview comparison, adjustable strength and optional automatic cropping of empty edges.
  • Custom aspect ratios and estimates of framing and resolution. Export to a pixel width and height using fit or fill without stretching; 8- or 16-bit-per-channel PNG and TIFF, including batch export.
Organization and navigation
  • Folder tags with an identifier, name and color, independent of Rostros; create, edit, merge and filter by all, any or no tags.
  • Keyboard navigation, single-click selection and double-click or Enter to open. Original/edited views in catalog detail and Rostros, with return to the last selected photo when it belongs to the view.
Batch operations
  • Actions grouped by purpose: apply LUTs and presets, copy or reset blocks, classify by stars and color, and export. The form remembers the last confirmed action without running it automatically.
  • Renaming with previews, collision checks and preservation of associated files. Supported batches show progress, allow cancellation and recover pending tasks.
Motion and 3D depth
  • A three-step workflow with playback, pause and sequence scrubbing before export. Improved edges and background reconstruction, with warnings for unreliable maps and conservative motion limits.
  • Manual map correction with closer, farther and smoothing brushes, explanations, comparison and undo. Restablecer mapa restores the earlier base even after corrections have been applied.
Performance, usability and security
  • Thumbnails in Rostros and catalog detail are limited to the visible area and a margin, releasing images and cancelling unnecessary loads. Lower resource usage when opening RAW folders, reuse of recent originals and cache improvements.
  • Optional automatic ComfyUI startup, editor diagnostic messages hidden by default and more consistent controls. Migration to .NET 10 and dependency updates to fix known vulnerabilities.
From RAW processing to your own style.
PREVIOUS VERSION · 1.0.0.23

PhotoManager 1.0.0.23

This release brings improvements to Catalog, Faces, RAW navigation, editing and process recovery. Browse improvements and fixes grouped by area.

Catalog and “Ver contenido”
  • Overview of folders, photographs, RAW, edited and analysed images; pinned folders and restored detail views when returning from Thumbnails.
  • Gallery grouped by year and month, chronological sorting, expandable groups and two to six photos per row, also adjustable with Ctrl and the mouse wheel.
  • Search and filters for format, editing, analysis, faces, rating and colour. “Quitar filtros” resets the view in one action.
  • Compact header with centred metrics and “Volver al catálogo”, “Abrir miniaturas”, “Refrescar” and “Ayuda” actions.
  • Automatic indexing when a folder has no indexed photographs yet. Shows photos found and current-stage progress when the total is known; indexing runs in the background.
  • Folder cards let you rename folders and remove them from the catalog. Renaming updates paths; removal asks for confirmation and preserves photos and PME files.
  • Removes entries for files that no longer exist and loads star, colour, editing, analysis and face indicators on demand.
Faces, tags and timeline
  • Global dashboard with metrics and filters for person, tag, folder and status; photos grouped by capture date, or modification date when EXIF is unavailable.
  • Collapsible year and month groups, two to six uncropped photos per row and thumbnails loaded when each group opens.
  • Collapsible properties with saving for pending changes. Global tags can be created, renamed, coloured, activated, merged or deleted in “Gestionar etiquetas”.
  • Colour palette and custom picker, consistent tags in “Asignar rostro”, and migration of previous tags with a backup.
  • Persistent face index with progressive background updates; preserves data from disconnected drives and links people, photos and folders.
  • Undo assignments with Ctrl+Z and remove an assignment without deleting its face signature. Person, photo, filters and scroll position are restored on return.
Thumbnails, RAW and editing
  • Thumbnails with saved PME recipe adjustments, an editing badge and an “Original / Editada” selector with both views ready.
  • Progressive RAW loading: thumbnail, embedded preview and full development. Adjacent images are preloaded; preview and thumbnail caches are limited to 2 GB in total.
  • Navigation to the first, previous, next and last photo with a current/total counter. The editor respects filters and order, saving the recipe when changing photos.
  • Quick transitions keep the image visible; longer waits are indicated in the footer. Viewing and exporting use independent decoding.
  • Double-click opens the selected thumbnail in the editor. If the RAW file is still being prepared, the requested opening waits for full development.
  • Fixed RAW and ORF preview errors, memory spikes, RAW/JPEG pair updates and concurrent thumbnail generation.
  • Masks remain anchored to the same area when zooming, multiple selection avoids accidental double handling, and checkboxes sit outside the image.
Guided tours, navigation and processes
  • A 23-step main tour and dedicated help: four steps in Catalog, five in Ver contenido, and eight in Faces and Editor.
  • Demo data and images, previous/next navigation and Escape to exit. In Ver contenido, photos appear inside the tour card and search is demonstrated with a matching image.
  • Consistent header buttons and icons. Shared footer in Thumbnails, Catalog, Faces, Editor and Comparison with version, Processes and Settings.
  • Analysis continues in the background when changing folders; pending analyses, suggestions, local adjustments, identifications and face recognition resume after restarting.
  • Recoverable exports with destination validation and temporary-file writes; copy and move operations preserve associated files and verify their integrity before confirming.
  • Recovered, interrupted and blocked processes are clearly identified. A pending Recycle Bin operation requires renewed confirmation.
Distribution and documentation

Adds the MIT licence and privacy, security and code-signing policies, along with product metadata and the licence in the installer. Check each release’s notes for the signing status of its download.

Requirements: 64-bit Windows 10 and 11. The installer includes the required .NET runtime.
